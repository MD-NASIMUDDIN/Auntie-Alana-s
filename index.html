About Us

Meet Auntie Alana

Auntie Alana's Childminding provides warm, home-based childcare in Wrexham, Wales. Every day is filled with a mix of sensory play, imaginative games, outdoor time and seasonal activities, in a caring and familiar setting.

From soft play visits and nature-inspired sensory trays to community events and a weekly Parent & Toddler Group, Auntie Alana brings warmth and genuine care to every day.
